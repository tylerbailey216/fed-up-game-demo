# Illustrated mobile dashboard ? November 17, 2026

Desktop's authored cockpit is preserved. Phone/tablet portrait and touch landscape now use a purpose-built instrument deck instead of the generic rectangular speed/status card. The existing cockpit illustration supplies real hatching, vent trim, seams and warm molding. Its lower strip is sampled at its original aspect ratio; no concept sheet is cropped into runtime art. Gauges, needles, warning lights, buttons, steering rim and pedal ribs are live vector drawing based on the supplied Driving Simulator UI Asset Sheet and Comic Truck Driving Simulator HUD. No additional AI artwork was needed. Static vector plates, icons, dial faces, stitching and steering-wheel art rasterize once into cached high-resolution textures; live needles, readouts, signals and input remain independent. This avoids sending hundreds of anti-aliased decorative line draws every frame. The supplied Barlow/Bangers font imports use 2x oversampling to keep lettering crisp under the larger landscape scale.

Portrait has a shallow FedUp/energy/objective rail, four compact illustrated utility buttons, a smaller GPS and a bottom instrument deck. mph, RPM, fuel, temperature, gear and vehicle warning states remain live. Steering, pedals, direction selector and held handbrake/drift remain independent touch controls. On foot, the instrument area becomes recovery progress and the wheel becomes a movement pad; auto-collection and camera gestures are unchanged. All manual vehicle functions remain available in SETUP / Vehicle. Hold VIEW to recenter; tap to cycle; swipe clear road space to orbit/tilt.

The full-width clear band between the toolbar/GPS and instrument deck is 60?63% of usable viewport height on the tested tall phone proportions (720?1228 and 360?640). This excludes Safari's browser chrome. Tablet and short landscape use a shorter deck with the controls at the edges. The 3D projection follows viewport aspect without stretching. Landscape is recommended in the menu but never forced, and no persistent rotate-device popup is added. The rear mirror remains desktop-only in compact layouts to avoid a second scene render.

Shared hud_layout.gd geometry drives HUD drawing, touch hit areas, camera gesture exclusions and recovery prompts. Compact messages still expire in the objective rail. Recovery/replay occupies a small reserved instrument area, with no overlap with steering or pedals. Manual expanded GPS/settings are deliberately user-controlled.

The old three-hit cap is removed. Each distinct collision episode drains 6?44 energy based on closing-speed severity; a light bump costs 6, and maximum severity costs 44. Cargo.damage is the authoritative accumulated damage (0?100); the HUD shows 1?damage/100. Diagnostic collision_count never gates disable or spill. Only damage at 100 shuts down the truck and releases exactly the stage quota once. Sustained wall contact cannot drain the bar repeatedly. Recovering all packages restores full energy and closes the doors as before. Tuning is centralized in cargo_gameplay.gd:impact_damage().

Validation: delivery suite covers five light bumps retaining 70% energy, positive-energy spill rejection, final depletion with diagnostic count one, actual separated wall impacts, single quota spill, no leakage, full walk-over recovery, repaired truck, reverse and completion. UI suite captures desktop, portrait phone, tablet and touch landscape and checks control bounds/no overlap, compact prompts, notification expiry and driving camera gestures. Browser controls suite exercises real touch/mouse input, five light bumps, reverse, orbit/recenter and twelve distant automatic pickups in four layouts. Screenshots are developer artifacts under docs/ui-illustrated-*.png and docs/controls-*.png; physical iPhone/Safari performance remains a separate check.
