# FedUp fleet and delivery foundation

The vehicle garage opens after loading, before entering gameplay. The original truck and Thanksgiving GOBBLE EXPRESS are available immediately. A completed Delivery Mayhem route unlocks the semi at 1 career delivery, tank at 3, and Santa sleigh at 5. Locked cards display the corresponding vehicle silhouette and a drawn padlock. Both modes use the same catalog, assets and progression. Zen Drive remains optional and has no required deliveries, cargo spills or permanent damage.

Choose a card, then DRIVE. Escape or MAIN MENU closes the garage. In a paused unfinished Mayhem run, resume the current delivery; changing vehicles is available after finishing. Zen can start a fresh drive with a different unlocked vehicle from Vehicle garage. Progress and selection save on this device/browser origin, not to a cross-device account. Clearing browser data removes that browser's career. The screen reports save failures instead of promising persistence.

The semi is a compact rigid tractor/trailer model with six live wheels and opening cargo doors; an articulated hitch is future work. The tank has tracked bodywork, twelve visible rolling road wheels and branded parcel storage. Its model is enlarged 1.5 times across and 1.45 times vertically/lengthwise, with a matching 4.5 x 4.2 x 7 m solid envelope, recovery clearance and camera footprint. It is larger than the semi, original truck and sleigh. All currently share the established handling/energy foundation, without separate performance tiers. All Mayhem vehicles retain screen-relative direction controls and the 10 m/s cap. Zen retains simulator steering and reverse. The truck and semi support the existing shared cockpit; tank, sleigh and turkey use exterior views.

GOBBLE EXPRESS is a giant living delivery turkey with a seated FedUp worker, layered fan/wing feathers, purple/orange parcel saddlebags, gold harness, parcels and branded cap. Its two hip/knee leg rigs follow actual distance travelled, returning to rest when stopped. The separately batched seated rider hides while the player is on foot and returns when remounted. It has no fake wheels, engine/tire noise, exhaust or hood smoke; ambience and shared gameplay feedback remain. It uses a 4 x 4.65 x 6 m solid envelope. This initial mount uses the shared driving and delivery rules; separate animal handling, vocals and special abilities are future work. The rider and turkey are articulated/procedural 3D interpretations, not newly drawn directional sprite frames.

The Santa sleigh includes six harnessed reindeer in three pairs, parcels, retracting road wheels and gold runners. All 24 legs use articulated 3D hip/knee gait and tucked flight poses; Santa remains seated. Gold traces connect the team. Its forward-extended 8.5 m solid footprint, recovery clearance and parked cutaway outline include the longer team, and the Mayhem camera follows the combined footprint's centre. V or the visible FLY / LAND button toggles low-altitude flight while retaining each mode's horizontal controls. It rises about four meters from takeoff, collides with the world and descends physically on landing. Land before exiting or completing a delivery. Flight is an initial bounded hover implementation, not a full aviation simulation. These are 3D figures, not directional animation sheets. Damage and resets cancel flight safely.

Flight loops the user's `D:/mixkit-sleigh-bells-584.wav`, copied byte-for-byte to `assets/audio/sleigh_bells.wav` (4.249 seconds, stereo 44.1 kHz; SHA-256 `aa3650d3b016edb436584894d392b9d3db00b14d8e643fa7c8b05cdd18161edf`). It shares master volume, continues during descent and stops on touchdown, cancellation or leaving gameplay. The existing city ambience remains. `sleigh_snow.gd` creates 72 tumbling, six-armed solid 3D snowflake meshes in one MultiMesh cloud. They fall and drift around the airborne team without rotating their falling trajectories with steering. No image generation, billboard sprites, physics bodies or road accumulation are used. The cloud freezes with pause and clears on touchdown/cancellation; its count is the same on native PC and touch/browser layouts.

`delivery_ledger.gd` assigns stable IDs to a fixed package manifest and a unique job. Energy depletion marks that same manifest lost once. Walk-over recovery immediately credits each package once; the existing shimmer-to-vehicle presentation remains. A completed route and full stop produce one receipt. `fleet_progress.gd` persists credited job IDs and rejects duplicate receipts. Each replay creates a new job and fixed manifest. Future multiple destinations, ordered delivery jobs, payouts and vehicle-specific capacity can build on this ledger; they are not implemented in this milestone.

## Verification

`--fleet-test` checks fixed manifest identity, missing and duplicate pickups, route/stop requirements, receipt deduplication, unlock thresholds, save/reload persistence, and real direction-input route laps with all five vehicles, plus sleigh ascent/landing, tank footprint and the turkey's leg rig. It uses isolated progress and removes its test save. The save workflow and Pages CI both run it. `tools/browser_fleet.cjs` exercises real card/DRIVE clicks, locked states, all five live models and flight at phone portrait/landscape, tablet and desktop sizes. Its explicit `?qa=fleet` fixture and review camera are ephemeral and never loaded in normal play. The garage uses two columns in portrait and five in landscape, with a scrollable vehicle area on short displays and fixed departure actions.

The garage return regression is covered by `tools/browser_garage_return.cjs`: complete a semi delivery fixture, use the report's actual garage button, reopen repeatedly, rotate the viewport, and check illustration size, action bounds and unchanged career progress. It includes 3x-density phone, 2x tablet and desktop layouts. The original bug reproduced as 290-high cards collapsing to 120 after returning. Card space now uses font measurements at the intended width rather than a hidden wrapped label's cached minimum. Web touch sizing uses canvas CSS pixels rather than backing pixels, and landscape puts DRIVE and MAIN MENU side by side to retain illustration space. Vehicle scene reloads are deferred until the input event finishes. This correction does not alter career saves, unlock thresholds or gameplay.
