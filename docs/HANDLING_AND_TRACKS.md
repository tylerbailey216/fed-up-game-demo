# Steering, tire trails and physical crashes

The lighter default uses a steering input rate of 6.5/s, return rate 8/s and initial countersteer rate 9/s. Yaw response uses `0.095 / (1 + abs(speed) * 0.16)` with signed speed. This improves low/mid-speed turning while retaining speed taper, grip recovery, reverse steering and low-speed barrier escape. The ring regression's feedback driver uses the same gain for its feedforward calculation; it still drives with input actions rather than setting poses during the circuit.

`tire_marks.gd` owns an independent world-space MultiMesh of at most 2,048 strips. On tire squeal/stress, four downward rays find flat static support and connect contacts 0.16-2 m apart. Wider gaps, no contact, non-flat support, resets, recovery and crashes break connections. Birth time/intensity are instance data; `tire_marks.gdshader` fades strips from age 4 to age 7 using a paused gameplay clock. There are no permanent per-frame nodes or downloaded tread textures.

Severe crash response in `van.gd` creates a real compound RigidBody3D from the driving hull, disables the CharacterBody shapes, and transfers reduced tangential motion/recoil plus contact-dependent pitch/roll energy. Its 3,200 kg mass, lower center of mass, friction 0.85, bounce 0.02 and damping settle the truck through physics. `vehicle_visuals.gd` follows that transform instead of rotating it on a timer. A counted tip is actual up-axis dot world-up <0.2, once per crash. Recovery waits for low linear/angular motion; an upright non-rollover can recover automatically. Root menu transitions explicitly freeze and restore crash velocities.

Normal driving remains an arcade controller, and wall climbing remains assisted. Damage is still the three-rollover rule, not structural deformation or a mechanical damage model. Physical outcomes can vary with impact geometry; not every high-speed collision flips the truck. Current mirrored 34 m/s angled barrier cases tip onto their sides around 90 degrees, while the frontal case settles upright with no counted flip. Human keyboard and collision-feel acceptance remains necessary.

Run `--handling-test` with `--headless --fixed-fps 60` for mirrored steering response/centering/countersteer, marks on three surfaces, hard stops/drift/turns, ordinary driving/airborne suppression, paused fading, seven-second expiry, frontal versus oblique crashes, bounded lift, pause and menu momentum preservation. `--handling-capture` writes `tire-marks.png`, `tire-marks-faded.png` and `physical-rollover.png`. Existing driving/crash/traffic/off-road suites also cover the integrated behavior.
