# Pedestrian, vehicle, street and energy update

The supplied pedestrian/vehicle sheets now drive the downtown cast: eight illustrated civilian identities with 12-frame walks in four directions, and fourteen modeled vehicle designs with live wheel articulation. The route pool holds 48 citizens and 30 vehicles; local activity is bounded by distance. The driver and every civilian keep the same cartoon, nonlethal reaction rules. Artwork generation used the built-in ImageGen tool and its final prompts are recorded in `LINEUP_ART_PROMPTS.json`; Blender model sources are included under `assets/source/traffic/`.

The truck HUD now uses an energy bar across desktop, phone and tablet. Its full/middle/low states are green/gold/red. Three distinct qualifying crashes still empty it, disable the truck and release exactly the required twelve boxes once. Completing walk-over recovery restores the truck and energy. A stationary truck is solid to the playable worker, and mobile reverse retains selected direction while gas is held.

Raised sidewalks and the entrance island share visible/physical geometry. Curbs provide suspension movement and bump audio without damage. Tree trunks and sign/signal poles are solid; grass and soft vegetation provide drag/roughness, with bushes bending near the truck. Sidewalks are clipped at road junctions. See `STREET_CONTACTS_AND_ENERGY.md` for the simplified physical model.

The regression suite checks actual two-way curb crossings and vertical lift, no curb damage, solid tree contact, all eight identities/fourteen vehicle types, axle preservation, citizen reaction/recovery, energy after each physical crash, fixed cargo quota and collection/finish. Browser touch/keyboard tests cover reverse, distant auto-pickups and energy restore on four screen layouts. Performance samples are desktop Chrome emulation on Intel UHD 620, not physical phone validation; the dense wide mobile sample remains below a stable 30 FPS target. See `PERFORMANCE_BUDGETS.md` for measured results and limits.
