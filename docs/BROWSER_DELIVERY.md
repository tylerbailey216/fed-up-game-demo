# Browser and delivery update — November 16, 2026

Current November 17 behavior supersedes the historical notes below: impact severity drains the life bar and zero energy alone releases cargo; walk-over sparkle collection replaces carrying/depositing; this demo ends after the first 12-package route. Mobile has a dedicated illustrated dashboard. See [current mobile and damage rules](MOBILE_DASHBOARD.md) and [README](../README.md).

Default location: training ring connected to a 1.3 km OpenStreetMap-derived downtown Cleveland street blockout. 1,238 road/path sections, 227 building footprints, Public Square park paths, an original procedural Terminal Tower landmark and eight physical traffic cars. Independence remains the second map. Street access and traffic rules are simplified for the game.

The truck GLB has a hollow cargo bay and two rear-door pivots. The first two registered collisions after training keep cargo secured. The third disables the truck and ejects the entire fixed stage quota once: 12, then 16, then 20 packages. There is no timed or distance-based cargo leak. On foot the worker carries one package to the truck or purple depot. The third distinct crash disables the delivery truck, with automatic safe exit once settled. Position reset cannot bypass a spilled load.

The playable worker uses a transparent illustrated animation atlas prepared from the supplied character sheet, with walk/run/carry cycles, directional idle and rotating follow camera. The original sheet remains local. The finished loading page and title menu share Cleveland key art generated from the supplied worker, truck and cockpit references. Bangers and Barlow Condensed font files include their OFL licenses.

On-foot repair: the training ring and downtown use one continuous ground collider, removing overlapping floors that could consume all character sliding attempts. Walking tests cross the training exit and a mapped Public Square footway in both directions. Front/back walking and carrying use a dedicated animation atlas; side movement retains the supplied side cycles. F or RETURN BOX explicitly returns held cargo at the truck rear or depot; no automatic proximity deposit occurs. The existing package body is stowed visibly rather than spawning a replacement. Both native launchers import changed assets before starting, so a D: source mirror can rebuild its local Godot cache instead of failing at font preloads.

Crash counting repair: the prior 0.4-second sound cooldown allowed sustained wall contact to count as three mission collisions. Reproduced with six seconds of held throttle. Mission damage now follows a separate crash serial, latched until the truck has moved 2.5 meters from the impact and its clearance hull has stayed free of obstacles for 0.65 seconds. Audio scrape/bump repeats remain independent. Delivery-stage totalled state uses three distinct crashes; early rollovers remain recoverable. The delivery regression physically drives all three stages into a wall with held throttle and also checks a severe first impact.

Camera repair: shared sphere sweeps prevent camera penetration into physical walls. Worker obstruction tries a clear overhead view; close, raised and overhead presets are available, with continuous orbit and bounded zoom. Driving adds a raised chase view. Tests cover wall and truck obstruction, preset cycling and touch orbit. Rendered captures use `--presentation-check`. [Godot 4.5 shape sweeps](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.5/classes/class_physicsdirectspacestate3d.html#class-physicsdirectspacestate3d-method-cast-motion) supply the safe movement fraction; the runtime subtracts a near-plane clearance margin.

Responsive layouts include portrait phone, portrait/landscape tablet and desktop. The illustrated desktop cockpit retains uniform scaling. Smaller screens use a separate live instrument layout. The actual 3D camera projection follows viewport aspect; multi-finger controls track steering and pedals independently. Menus scroll on short screens. The rear mirror does not render in compact mode to reduce work.
